How it worksBrowse or filter the startlist, heart the riders you fancy to shortlist them (Riders: Favourites shows only those), then add up to ten riders in your predicted finish order, plus three wildcards. Save whenever you like; you can keep changing your picks until the deadline. Keep alternative lists as templates: start one with +, or right-click a tab (double-tap on a phone) to duplicate it; drag tabs to reorder them. Templates save themselves; only your final prediction is scored. After the race, compare your score and prediction with the league.

Scoring rewards Top 10 accuracy: lower-ranked riders earn more, and the better you call the podium, the more you score (how scoring works).
